Darshan & Seva

What changed, and why

Splash — the thesis
The stock photo, the two inset gold rectangles and the three bouncing dots are gone; those three together were what made it read as a template. The sky is painted from the theme's own tokens, so there's no image decode before first paint.
The signature
The gold mandala blooms open as a halo behind the tilak, which draws itself in like a lamp being lit. The same halo persists, small and faint, behind the tilak in the app bar — that's the one thread tying the opening screen to every screen after it.
The wait means something
The dot spinner is replaced by a breathing invocation. A bespoke screen shouldn't end in the same loader every app ends in.
Inside the tiles
Each icon now stands in a pointed temple alcove on a small gold threshold, lit from within. This is the third and last sanctioned use of the arch, alongside the active nav tab and the section crown — its scarcity is what keeps it a signature.
One repeated device
That gold threshold under the tile icon reappears under the active nav tab. Repeating one small detail in two places turns it into the app's vocabulary rather than a flourish.
Dawn wash
Home opens with warm lamplight spilling from under the header instead of starting on cold paper — the morning counterpart to the splash's midnight. One gradient layer, no extra DOM.
Comfortaa, everywhere
Every Latin role — lockup, titles, headings, body, UI, verse — is now Comfortaa. Cinzel, Marcellus, Inter and Cormorant Garamond are still declared in fonts.css but nothing references them, so a revert is a one-file edit. Devanagari is untouched: it stays on Noto Sans / Noto Serif Devanagari, and the Latin faces are clamped by unicode-range so Hindi can never fall into a font that has no glyphs for it.
Two knock-on fixes
Comfortaa has no true italic, so every italic in the app is gone — synthesised oblique on a rounded face looks broken. And hierarchy used to come from serif-vs-sans contrast; with one family that signal disappears, so section titles now carry 600 and card titles 500. Tracking was pulled in too (0.34em → 0.24em on the splash subtitle, 0.20em → 0.16em on eyebrows) because Comfortaa already has wide sidebearings where Cinzel's capitals needed the air.

Dark mode

What was actually wrong
The plumbing was always fine — darkMode:"class", the .dark toggle, the ink tokens, the night-sky page floor. What was missing was coverage: several hundred elements carried a light utility with no dark: counterpart, so they stayed light-on-light. Grey body text became grey-on-navy; white cards stayed white. 731 pairings were added.
Why the substitution is safe
The ink ramp is luminance-matched step-for-step against Tailwind's slate — only the hue moves toward Krishna blue. Swapping ink for slate at the same step therefore can't change a contrast ratio, so even a mis-aimed substitution is visually neutral instead of a regression. The ratios below are measured live in your browser, not asserted.

The two judgment calls. bg-white could not be automated — white is deliberate wherever it sits on something dark, coloured or photographic. Of 32 surfaces, 14 were paired and 18 left white, including the donation QR plate (it must stay white to scan) and the PDF render canvas (white is the paper; darkening it makes black ink unreadable). And hover states were a separate sweep: a light hover tint on a dark card reads as a white flash, which matters here because the app runs on Android TV with D-pad focus.
