About Us

Shree Chaitanya Prem Bhakti Sangh is a Gaudiya Vaishnava community formed to preach the Holy Names of the Lord across the northwestern and central regions of India, following the teachings of Shri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.

Prem Bhakti App

Prem Bhakti is the official website and app of Shri Chaitanya Prem Bhakti Sangh. It gives devotees easy access to daily darshan, bhajans, kirtans, lectures, spiritual books, the Vaishnav calendar, temple timings and festival updates, all in one place.
